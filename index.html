    Target percentile

    One honest limit up front: CAT's overall percentile comes from your combined scaled score, not from a formula that combines sectional percentiles — that exact mapping isn't published by CAT or anyone else, so no tool can give you a guaranteed-official split. What this gives you instead is a statistically grounded estimate: it treats percentiles as points on a standard bell curve (the same principle behind every score-vs-percentile table you'll see), and works out what each section would need to average out to your overall goal — asking less of sections you're already strong in and more of the ones dragging you down. "Current standing" below is based on full mocks once you've logged any — a mock under real time pressure across all three sections is a more honest read than an isolated sectional drill — and only falls back to sectional-only data until your first mock is in.
